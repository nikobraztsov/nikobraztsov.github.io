---
title: "Nikita Obraztsov Attended the AIM-CC 2026 Summer School in Astana"
date: 2026-08-15
tags: ["news", "research"]
---

In August, Nikita Obraztsov took part in the AI & Methods in Computational Communication summer school, organised by Kazakhstan Sociology Lab at Nazarbayev University in Astana. The programme covered network analysis, polarisation research, and the use of AI agents in social science research. Nikita presented a poster on his agent-based model and gave a closing talk on the final day of the school.
