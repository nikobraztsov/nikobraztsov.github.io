On 15 October 2026, Nikita Obraztsov presented at the IX Congress of the Russian Religious Studies Society. The talk, titled "The Operationalisation of Religiosity as an Unresolved Problem in Contemporary Religious Studies: A Computational Perspective," reported the first stage of an ongoing research project.

![Presenting at the IX Congress of the Russian Religious Studies Society](/images/nikita-presentation.jpg)

A corpus of approximately 30,000 abstracts containing the term *religiosity* was assembled from Q1+ journals via OpenAlex (2010–2026) and clustered using k-means. Eight clusters emerged, covering domains from psychological health surveys and clinical research to political attitudes, cross-national comparisons, and Islamic finance. The results support the argument, advanced by the late M.Yu. Smirnov, that the field is so heterogeneous that a unified approach to operationalising religiosity is neither current practice nor a near-term prospect.
