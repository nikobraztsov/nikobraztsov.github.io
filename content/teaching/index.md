I have been teaching religious studies to a general audience since 2022. My recent public lectures also cover computational methods in sociology. My teaching ranges from introductory surveys to courses built around specific research questions and scholarly debates.

## Teaching and course mentoring

**Pravoe Polusharie Introverta (Introvert), online education platform — guest lecturer in religious studies (since 2022) and course mentor.**

As of July 2026, my lectures accounted for 139 entries in the platform's active catalogue. They include surveys of Christianity, Islam, Judaism and Buddhism, a series on new religious movements in nineteenth-century America, and a seven-lecture course on Émile Durkheim and the sociology of religion.

I also mentor students on the professional retraining programme in Cultural Studies (*Culturologist*). The programme comprises 90 lectures and 250 academic hours, covering cultural history, cultural theory and applied cultural studies. My role includes supporting students as they work through the programme.

[Lecturer profile and catalogue (in Russian)](https://new.artforintrovert.ru/lecturer/63c79ad8f3cf3a0028f9b335/)

**Private tutoring in social studies, 2020–2022.** I prepared school students for subject olympiads in social studies.

## Public lectures

**How Computer Simulations Can Help Sociologists: Four Studies Worth Knowing** — School of Computational Social Sciences, European University at St. Petersburg, 25 November 2026.

Using four published studies, I discussed how agent-based models can help explain opinion polarisation, changes in marriage patterns and the role of network structure in epidemic transmission. The lecture also considered competing explanations and the limitations of simulations.
