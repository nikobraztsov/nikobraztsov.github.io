In August, I took part in the AI & Methods in Computational Communication summer school, organised by Kazakhstan Sociology Lab at Nazarbayev University in Astana. The programme covered network analysis, polarisation research, and the use of AI agents in social science research. I presented a poster on my agent-based model and gave a closing talk on the final day of the school.
