This summer Nikita Obraztsov gave his first talk at a major international conference: the 8th European Conference on Social Networks (EUSN 2026), held in Norrköping, Sweden. The presentation introduced an agent-based model of how religious communities maintain their religiosity under secularising pressure. The talk was delivered remotely.
