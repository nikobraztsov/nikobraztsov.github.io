I spoke with Daria Pavlova for the online magazine Ambivert about my career in religious studies, the relationship between religious studies and theology, and the future of religion.

A large part of our conversation focused on how to interpret survey data on youth religiosity in Russia and demographic trends in Europe. We discussed the distinction between religious identity and practice, the intergenerational transmission of religion, and the limits of long-term demographic projections. We also talked about religious syncretism, new religious movements, and the individualisation of religious beliefs and practices.
